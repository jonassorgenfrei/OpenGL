Changes in this repository:
- Center-cropped and resampled to 1024 x 1024 using Lanczos (Pillow 12.0.0).
- RGB remains a photograph. An artificial left-to-right 8-bit alpha ramp was
  added to the bottom quarter to compare BC2/DXT3 and BC3/DXT5 transparency.
- Stored as landscape.png and a top-row-first uncompressed RGBA8 DDS reference.
- BC1/DXT1 (RGB), BC2/DXT3 and BC3/DXT5 use the sample's small offline encoders.
- ASTC 4x4, 8x8, 12x12 use Arm astcenc 5.7.0, LDR linear profile, medium preset.
- preview_*.png contain offline ASTC decodes for drivers without ASTC support.
- No AI-generated image content.

Reproduction: scripts/prepare_texture_compression.py and the optional
Texture_Compression_Prepare target. Normal builds and runs need neither Python
nor an ASTC encoder, because the prepared assets are included.
